Privacy Policy — HanU-X

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CGIOCEVEREST PRIVATE LIMITED (“we”, “us”) collects and uses information in the HanU-X athlete monitoring app.

Registered office: B, 21/03, Kamal Vihar, Rajendra Nagar, Raipur, Chhattisgarh 492001, India. Contact: rahulpandey@cgioceverest.com

1. Who the app is for

HanU-X is used by athletes, coaches, and organisation staff for training, wellness, nutrition, performance, and related sports-science monitoring. It is not directed at children under 13.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Account details (name, email, role such as athlete/coach/admin)
  • Organisation and team membership
  • Wellness and training check-ins (for example mood, sleep, pain, readiness)
  • Nutrition and meal logs
  • Performance test or session logs
  • Documents or images you choose to upload (for example health or training files); we extract only fields needed for the features you use, after you confirm where required
  • Device and technical logs needed to run and secure the app

3. How we use information

We use this information to provide HanU-X features: athlete monitoring dashboards, coach/organisation views you are authorised for, calculations and reports, customer support, security, and improving the product. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Sharing

Data may be visible to coaches or organisation admins who manage your team, according to your organisation’s setup. We use trusted service providers (for example cloud hosting and authentication such as Supabase) to run the app. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored with our cloud providers with access controls. You should keep your login details private. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Retention

We keep information while your account and organisation need it for the service, and as required for legal or security reasons. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us; organisation-owned records may remain under the organisation’s instructions.

7. Your choices

You can update profile information in the app where available, and contact us to ask about access, correction, or deletion subject to applicable law and organisation policies.

8. Health-related information

HanU-X may process wellness, recovery, and related sports-health style inputs you or your organisation enter. This is for athlete monitoring and coaching workflows, not for medical diagnosis or emergency care. If you upload health documents, treat them carefully and only share what your organisation requires.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the app after updates means you accept the revised policy.
