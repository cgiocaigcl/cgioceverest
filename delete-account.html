CGIOCEVEREST PRIVATE LIMITED — HanU-X athlete monitoring app

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to cgioceverest@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: HanU-X account deletion request
  3. Include the email address you use to sign in to HanU-X, and your full name

We will verify the request and delete your account and associated personal data from our systems.

What is deleted

  • Your login account
  • Your profile details in HanU-X
  • Wellness, nutrition, performance, and document data linked to your athlete profile (where we control it)

What may be kept

  • Records your organisation is legally required to keep
  • Security or fraud-prevention logs for a limited time
  • Aggregated data that no longer identifies you

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your request (sooner when possible).

Partial data deletion (without deleting your account)

Email cgioceverest@gmail.com and describe which data you want removed. We will respond with what we can delete while keeping your account active.
